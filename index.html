The workshop

Walter Craffonara

Pedal builder and electronics engineer, Austria

CRAFFONARA FX is a one-man workshop. By day I design automotive electronics; in the evening the same care goes into guitar pedals. Every board is laid out, soldered, measured and played by me before it leaves the bench.

What sets the builds apart is control: relays instead of mechanical switches, two pots where the original had one, and MIDI on every pedal so a whole board changes sound with one message.

Get in touch

Tell me what you want to build, mod or fit with MIDI. A rough idea is enough: the pedal model, what should switch, and how you want to control it.

I usually reply within one or two days with questions, options and a quote.
